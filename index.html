About

The Hope Club is a monthly faith-based mail club created to bring hope, encouragement, and Jesus into whatever season of life you're walking through. It was born during one of the hardest seasons of my life, losing my father. In the middle of my own grief, I wanted to turn some of that pain into something that could bring hope to someone else.

Each month, you'll receive a personal letter and a prayer from me, created to encourage you right where you are, whether you're healing, waiting, grieving, starting over, or simply need a little encouragement.

The heart behind The Hope Club is simple: to give you something meaningful to hold onto, encourage your faith, and remind you that you've got this, no matter what.

Xo, Fiona

Good to know

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I contact The Hope Club?

For questions about your membership, mailing address, or monthly envelope, email thehopeclubmail@gmail.com.

What does membership cost?

The Hope Club is $9 per month, with U.S. shipping included. Each month, members receive one personal letter and one audio prayer centered on grief, life after loss, and how Jesus is carrying me through it.

What comes in each mailing?

Every monthly envelope includes one personal letter sharing honest thoughts about grief, faith, and learning to live without my father, along with a card to scan for that month's audio prayer — a spoken prayer you can listen to whenever you need it.

Occasionally, I may include a small surprise, but the heart of the club will always be the letter and the prayer.

Who is The Hope Club for?

The Hope Club was created primarily for those walking through grief, but the writing may also speak to anyone navigating heartbreak, change, loneliness, or another difficult season. You do not have to be experiencing the same kind of loss as me to join.

When will my envelope be mailed?

Join by the last day of the month at 11:59 PM Eastern Time to receive the following month's mailing. Envelopes are prepared and sent during the first week of each month.

Is tracking included?

No. Hope Club envelopes are sent through USPS as stamped letter mail, so tracking is not included. Please allow enough time for USPS delivery, especially during holidays or periods of postal delay.

Is shipping included?

Yes. Standard shipping is included for members within the United States. At launch, The Hope Club will ship only within the United States.

Will my subscription renew automatically?

Yes. After joining, your membership will renew automatically each month until you cancel. Your renewal date will be shown during checkout and inside your customer account.

Can I cancel my membership?

Yes. You may cancel before your next renewal date to avoid being charged for the following month's mailing. Once a monthly order has renewed and preparation has begun, that order cannot be canceled or refunded.

Can I update my address or payment information?

Yes. You can update your mailing address and payment information through your customer account. Please make any address changes before the monthly cutoff so your envelope is sent to the correct place.

What happens if I enter the wrong address?

Please double-check your address before completing checkout. If your envelope is returned because the address was incorrect or incomplete, you will be responsible for the additional postage needed to mail it again.

Do you accept returns or offer refunds?

Because each month's mailing is prepared specifically for active members, all sales are final. If your envelope arrives damaged or there is a problem with your order, please contact me and I'll do my best to help.

Shipping & Policies

Shipping

Shipping is included in the membership price for all U.S. subscribers.

Each month's Hope Club envelope is carefully prepared and shipped during the first week of the month. You'll receive an email confirmation once your envelope has been mailed.

Returns & Exchanges

Because each Hope Club mailing is thoughtfully created for that month's members, all sales are final. We do not accept returns, exchanges, or refunds.

If your order arrives damaged, or you experience an issue with your shipment, please contact us — we'll do our best to make it right.

Incorrect Shipping Address

Please take a moment to carefully review your shipping address before placing your order.

If an order is returned to us due to an incorrect or incomplete mailing address, we'll contact you using the email provided at checkout. Any additional shipping costs required to resend the package are the responsibility of the customer.

Membership & Cancellations

The Hope Club is a monthly subscription that renews automatically until canceled.

You may cancel your membership at any time before your next renewal date to avoid being charged for the following month's envelope.

Once your monthly envelope has been processed or prepared for shipment, that month's order cannot be canceled, modified, or refunded.

Questions? Contact us — we're always happy to help.
